Your Profile

Save your details once

Entirely optional, and separate from your academy sign-in. Fill it in and the registration form and the Skills Gap check will fill themselves in for you, so you type your details once. Skip it and everything still works exactly as it does now — including your course progress, which is kept in your academy account either way.

Where things are kept Your course progress is saved to your academy account, on the academy’s server — so it follows you to your phone, survives this browser being cleared, and is there when your manager asks how you are getting on. The details on this page are something separate and smaller: a local shortcut that fills in forms for you. They stay in this browser, are never uploaded, and form no part of your learning record. On a shared or site machine, use “Delete my profile” at the bottom when you have finished, or the next person to open this page will see what you typed.
This is not your academy account If the academy has enrolled you on a course you have a sign-in as well, and that is where your progress, your modules and your record are held. Sign in, or go to your learning if you are already signed in.

Clearing this page

Deleting removes these form shortcuts from this browser straight away, and there is no copy anywhere else to restore them from. It does not touch your academy account, your place on a course, or any progress you have recorded — those are held in your account and are not affected by anything on this page.
