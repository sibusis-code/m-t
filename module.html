Inside the module

Activities, exercises and self-assessments from the workbook. You hand these to your facilitator and they go into your portfolio of evidence, signed off as your own work.

At the end of the module

A summative assessment set for this module. It is written and marked under the provider's assessment rules — the paper and its marking guide are not published anywhere, here included.

At the end of everything

The External Integrated Summative Assessment (EISA), set by the QCTO across the whole qualification. Passing individual modules is not the same as holding the certificate — the EISA is what awards it.
