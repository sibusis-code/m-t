About M&T Academy

Investing Our Profits, In Our People

M&T offers training in strategic leadership, technical and digital fluency, change management, and navigating organisational dynamics during periods of transformation. This is complemented by mentorship with senior leaders and technical experts, who provide guidance on career progression, help employees build resilience in the face of technological change, and advocate for their mentees' professional growth. These initiatives aren't just about individual development, they're about strengthening our talent pipeline as a whole, so that as the industry changes, our people change and grow with it.

We started with AI, and the catalogue now reaches well beyond it — technical, business, compliance, safety and admin — so whichever part of M&T you work in, there is something here aimed at your actual job.

  • A catalogue that reaches well beyond AI, into the work you actually do
  • A practical understanding of how AI tools work and where they help in your role
  • The confidence to use AI responsibly and effectively in your day-to-day work
  • Formal qualifications for the people who want to take it further
  • Skills mapped to the work we actually do at M&T, not abstract theory
  • No cost to you — every course is funded by the company
  • A route to grow your career here, whatever role you started in
What that means day to day

Construction and site teams, property and facilities management, leasing and sales, and the people who keep the offices running — all building real technical and AI capability. Practical courses that fit around the working day, and a credentialed path for anyone who wants to take it further.
