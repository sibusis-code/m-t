Accreditation

What the badges mean

Locally accredited — a real qualification on South Africa's NQF, so it counts with another employer and towards further study. Internationally recognised — a certificate from Google, Microsoft or Coursera; respected anywhere, but not on the NQF. Everything else is a short course.

Free international courses

These ones aren't ours. They're AI courses published by Google, Microsoft, Coursera and the University of Helsinki, and they're on this page because they're good and they cost nothing. You sign up directly with the provider, in your own name, and work through them at your own pace — no HR form, no waiting for an intake.

Two things to know before you start. The certificate comes from them, not from us, so when you finish add it to your profile and tell HR — otherwise it won't reach your record. And none of these sit on the NQF: they carry real weight with employers, but no NQF level and no credits towards a South African qualification. For that, the accredited qualifications above are the route.
